CASE STUDY // 2025

Luna Direction

_The Challenge

Luna Direction was about finding the 'soul' of the brand. We moved away from generic aesthetics toward a more artisanal, grounded visual language. This involved custom illustration sets, a new color palette inspired by twilight, and a comprehensive brand book. The new direction helped Luna secure partnerships with three major boutique retailers within the first quarter of implementation.
